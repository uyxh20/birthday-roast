Tags fill themselves in. Their case sets the output's case: {name} → Mochi as typed, {Name} → Mochi, {NAME} → MOCHI.

{name} {age} {ageth} {sign} {asign} {symbol} {element} {aelement} {fuel} {food} {origin} {from} {bday} {year}

Pronouns: {they} {them} {their} {theyre} {is} {has} {was} and {s} for verbs, e.g. {They} know{s} → "He knows" / "They know".
